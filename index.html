03 / PRINCIPLES

Confidence should
be earned.

01

Traceable decisions. Requirements, implementation, tests and outcomes belong to the same record.

02

Reproducibility. Evidence should identify revisions, workloads, configurations and limitations.

03

Security by design. Least privilege and rigorous review are part of delivery, not add-ons.

04

Honest outcomes. Rejected and inconclusive experiments are valuable when documented rigorously.
